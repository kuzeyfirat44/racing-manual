DTM Since 1984 · Germany

Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters

Germany's
Touring Car Titans

The DTM is Germany's home of touring car and GT racing. From the boxy homologation specials of the 1980s to today's GT3 cars, it has always been a battleground for Mercedes, BMW and Audi, fought out in front of huge crowds at the Nürburgring, Hockenheim and Norisring.

GT3 Era Begins
1984–1986
From Production Cars to DTM
Germany's touring car championship starts in 1984 as the Deutsche Produktionswagen-Meisterschaft and is renamed DTM in 1986. Factory-backed BMW M3s, Mercedes 190Es and Ford Sierras soon battle in front of huge crowds.
1988–1992
The Homologation Specials
Manufacturers build road-going "evolution" models to race. Klaus Ludwig wins the 1988 title in a Ford Sierra RS500 and the 1992 title in the winged Mercedes 190E Evo II, as Mercedes takes a 1-2-3 in the standings.
1993
Class 1 & The Alfa Invasion
New Class 1 rules allow far more freedom. Alfa Romeo arrives with the four-wheel-drive 155 V6 TI and Nicola Larini wins the title in its first season, winning 11 of 22 races.
1995–1996
ITC & Collapse
The series goes international as the ITC, but the technology and costs explode. Alfa Romeo and Opel pull out after 1996 and the championship collapses.
2000
Reborn as the Masters
The DTM returns as the Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters with V8 silhouette racers from Mercedes, Opel and Audi. Bernd Schneider wins the first two titles of the new era.
2019
Class One Turbo Era
DTM switches to 2.0-litre turbocharged four-cylinder engines under "Class One" rules shared with Japan's Super GT. René Rast wins back-to-back titles in 2019 and 2020 for Audi.
2021–2023
GT3 & A New Owner
With manufacturers leaving, DTM switches to GT3 cars in 2021, opening the grid to Mercedes-AMG, BMW, Porsche, Ferrari, Lamborghini and more. In 2023 the German motoring club ADAC takes over the series.

DTM Kings

1
Bernd Schneider
Germany
Mr. DTM

The most successful DTM driver ever. Bernd Schneider won the 1995 title in the original DTM, then four more after its revival, all with Mercedes. His 43 wins are still the series record, and in 1997 he also won the FIA GT Championship in the CLK GTR.

5× DTM Champion (1995, 2000, 2001, 2003, 2006)
43 DTM wins, the all-time record
1995 ITC Champion

3
Klaus Ludwig
Germany
König Ludwig

"King Ludwig" won almost everything in German motorsport. He took DTM titles with Ford and twice with Mercedes, won Le Mans three times with Porsche, and was crowned FIA GT champion in 1998. He came back to the DTM in 2000 at the age of 50.

3× DTM Champion (1988, 1992, 1994)
37 DTM wins, second all-time
3× Le Mans winner (1979, 1984, 1985)
1998 FIA GT Champion
33
René Rast
Germany
Title Machine

The best of the modern era. René Rast won the DTM title in 2017, his first full season, in an Audi RS 5. He won twice more in 2019 and 2020 in the Class One turbo era, and retired from the series in 2025 with 31 wins, third on the all-time list.

The winged legend. Mercedes built 502 road cars of the Evolution II so it could race, complete with its giant rear wing. In 1992 the Evo II won 16 of 24 DTM races and Mercedes drivers finished 1-2-3, with Klaus Ludwig the champion.
