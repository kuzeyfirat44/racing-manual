GT3 Racing &
Gentleman Racers

GT3 represents the golden balance between accessibility and performance. Introduced in 2006, the GT3 class allows wealthy privateers and gentleman drivers to race production-based supercars from Porsche, Ferrari, Audi, Mercedes, BMW and more all balanced through BoP (Balance of Performance) to ensure close, competitive racing.

The Philosophy of GT3

2006 - Present

GT3 was designed as the "customer racing" class: cars that wealthy amateurs and professional drivers could purchase from manufacturers and race competitively. Unlike GT1 or LMP1, GT3 focuses on close racing through Balance of Performance (BoP) regulations that adjust each car's weight, power, and aerodynamics to keep all manufacturers competitive. This creates incredible battles where driver skill matters more than outright speed.

Laurens Vanthoor

Porsche Ace

One of the finest GT drivers of his generation. Laurens Vanthoor first made his name with Audi, winning the Spa 24 Hours and the Nürburgring 24 Hours before becoming a Porsche factory driver. He has since won GT titles on both sides of the Atlantic, and in 2024 he became the WEC Hypercar World Champion with Porsche.

Nürburgring 24 Hours winner (2015)

Augusto Farfus
Brazil
BMW Legend

BMW's most loyal ace. Augusto Farfus has spent most of his career as a BMW factory driver, in touring cars, DTM and GT racing. He won the Nürburgring 24 Hours overall in 2010 and again in 2025, fifteen years apart. Known for his aggressive, committed style, he also won the FIA GT World Cup in Macau.

2× Nürburgring 24 Hours winner (2010, 2025)
2018 FIA GT World Cup winner (Macau)
2× Daytona 24 Hours GTLM class winner
2020 Intercontinental GT Challenge champion
1
Christopher Mies
Germany
Audi Master

Audi's GT3 specialist. Christopher Mies spent fifteen years as an Audi factory driver, winning the Nürburgring 24 Hours and the Bathurst 12 Hour with the R8 LMS. His feedback helped make the R8 LMS one of GT3's most successful cars. Mies is the model modern GT3 driver: fast, consistent and technically brilliant.

3× Nürburgring 24 Hours winner (2015, 2017, 2024)
2× Bathurst 12 Hour winner (2011, 2012)
2009 FIA GT3 European Champion
2× ADAC GT Masters champion (2016, 2021)
